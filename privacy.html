Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how MeetBot, an internal Telegram bot for creating Google Meet links, handles data.

Google data

MeetBot requests a single Google permission: creating Google Meet meeting spaces (meetings.space.created). It uses this permission only to create new meeting links when a team member asks for one. MeetBot does not read, store or share any Gmail messages, calendar events, contacts, files or other Google user data.

Telegram data

To control who can use the bot, MeetBot stores the Telegram user ID, display name and username of people who request access. For each meeting created, it logs the time, the requesting user's Telegram ID and username, and the meeting link.

How data is used and shared

This data is used only to operate the bot and manage access. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on a private server operated by the bot administrator. Users can ask for their data to be deleted at any time by contacting the administrator.

Contact

For questions about this policy or to request deletion of your data, contact wadetgcodes1@gmail.com.
